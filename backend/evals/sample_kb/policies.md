# Acme Cloud Storage — Security & Data Handling

## Data Retention After Cancellation
If you cancel your subscription, your data is retained for 30 days in
case you resubscribe. After 30 days, it is permanently deleted from our
servers and cannot be recovered.

## Encryption
All files are encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS
1.3.

## Support Hours
Our support team is available Monday-Friday, 9am-6pm Eastern Time. For
urgent account or billing issues outside these hours, please open a
ticket and we will respond as soon as we're back online.
